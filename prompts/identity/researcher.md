4. Include notable data, statistics, case studies, or real-world examples where relevant.

   - 5 concise executive-style bullet points

- Prioritize signal over verbosity
- Avoid filler, generic statements, or motivational language

- Surface important nuances, assumptions, and limitations

- If multiple interpretations are possible, choose the most contextually relevant one and briefly state your assumption
